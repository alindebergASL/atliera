# Direction B — Evidence Horizon Synthesis

## Design thesis

Direction B treats the Account Home as a semantic evidence horizon. It is not a timeline, progress bar, confidence score, activity stream, or dashboard. A continuous night field gives meaningful change and its bounded implication visual gravity, while connected nodes clarify how established context, changed fact, open meaning, and one safe next move relate.

The account name is compact metadata. The meaningful change—Relay Planner's introduction—is the center. The implication is visibly attached but remains a draft interpretation.

## Composition

- desktop uses a restrained orientation key outside one continuous evidence field;
- the changed statement owns the largest typographic mass;
- established context and still-open boundary occupy separate semantic chambers;
- fine orthogonal rules and nodes communicate lineage, never completion or confidence;
- recommendation lands on mineral paper at the edge of the dark horizon;
- on mobile, the design deliberately recomposes to changed → next → established → open, honoring insight-first reading rather than mechanically stacking desktop columns;
- trust remains a distinct quiet strip after the semantic field.

## Atliera-specific reusable behaviors

1. **Semantic horizon field:** meaningful change and implication live in one unmistakable evidence environment.
2. **Evidence current:** semantic nodes and connecting strokes bind basis to consequence without scores.
3. **Boundary chamber:** the still-open state visibly contains what the evidence cannot establish.
4. **Next-move landing:** one paper landing resolves out of the evidence field into a bounded human direction.

## Interaction

- no-plan primary: `Review source basis` → exact meaningful-change evidence;
- admitted-plan primary: `View existing meeting plan` → admitted plan only;
- in the plan state, a quiet `View source basis` node remains statement-local;
- the open-node action distinguishes evidence plus assumptions;
- desktop disclosure is a right-edge drawer;
- mobile disclosure is a bottom sheet;
- Close and Escape restore focus to the invoking node/action.

## Responsive behavior

At 1440/1280 the horizon uses three semantic zones without becoming a card grid. At tablet it becomes a two-column change center with one next landing. At 390/320 it is reordered for insight-first comprehension; the primary action remains visible, evidence is not detached from the changed statement, and no horizontal overflow occurs.

## Known compromises and production risks

- The dark horizon is more dramatic and may feel less quiet than Direction A for long reading sessions.
- If reused carelessly, the connected line could be mistaken for workflow or progress; the semantic labels and no-score rule are non-negotiable.
- Mobile reorders established/open context after the central change and next action; accessibility order is intentional but requires future user validation.
- The plan sheet remains content-dense because all admitted fields are preserved.
- This is static synthetic-fixture code, not production data binding or user research.

## Advisory tradeoff

Direction B offers the stronger ownable Atliera grammar and fastest five-second differentiation from merged C1. Its cost is higher visual drama and a stricter need to govern the evidence horizon so it never mutates into status visualization or dashboard ornament.
