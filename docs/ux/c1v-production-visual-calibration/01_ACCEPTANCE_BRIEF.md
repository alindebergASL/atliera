# C1V-01 acceptance brief

Disposition terms: `PASS`, `PASS WITH NON-BLOCKING POLISH`, or `HOLD`.

## Governing rule

> **Answers first. Evidence on demand. Machinery by invitation.**

A competent first-time viewer should understand the account, meaningful change, possible implication, next move, and exact basis without encountering internal machinery.

## Shared semantic lock

Both directions preserve:

- account: Citrine Works; `fictional fixture` is quiet metadata;
- account context: a fictional operations-software company serving regional planning teams;
- exact meaningful change: Relay Planner introduction on July 14, 2026 after an approved deployment review;
- draft interpretation: exception handling is a plausible discovery focus;
- draft recommendation: targeted exception-workflow meeting brief for internal review;
- source identity, publisher, source-date uncertainty, retrieval context, support and non-support boundaries;
- attributed—not independently source-backed—origin state;
- not-reviewed, freshness-not-established, unsaved, unapproved, and undelivered states;
- no-plan evidence-review action and admitted-plan existing-plan action;
- all three admitted questions and the combined close criterion.

No wording strengthens attribution, review, freshness, customer intent, recommendation, or durability.

## Direction A acceptance

Direction A passes only if it reads as a composed premium answer rather than a report in cards. The interpretation is the intellectual hero, account identity is compact, question-led logic remains visible, exact support is stitched to the change, and the one primary action is obvious at every required width.

## Direction B acceptance

Direction B passes only if meaningful change and implication form the visual center, the evidence horizon communicates semantic orientation rather than progress/confidence/workflow, support nodes stay attached to consequential statements, and the stronger drama does not destroy calm comprehension or mobile reachability.

## Visual evaluation threshold

Each direction requires:

- at least 85/100 total;
- at least 20/25 distinctiveness;
- at least 17/20 calmness;
- at least three reusable Atliera-specific behaviors;
- no non-compensable blocker.

Scores are agent/expert calibration for owner selection. They are not user research, customer readiness, or the zero-training user gate.
