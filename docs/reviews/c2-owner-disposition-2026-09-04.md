## University of Utah

**1. Useful — are the thesis and most meaningful change commercially useful?**
For: The thesis nails a real, time-bound commercial context — a legislatively forced $60M three-year reinvestment with a board-approved Year Two plan and a named ~$4.94M Responsible AI line, plus separate Redtail ($50M) and UHAIV ($18.6M + $15M) AI investments. For an AI-infrastructure seller this is about as good as public-evidence account context gets: money, mandate, timeline, and named programs. The "why it matters" correctly frames it as a discovery area, not an opportunity claim.
Against: The thesis is dense — three distinct funding streams compressed into one sentence — and the most meaningful change (the six reinvestment lines) is the least AI-specific of the three streams. Arguably Redtail/UHAIV are the more commercially interesting change for an infrastructure seller, and they sit in "Still open" rather than headline.

**2. Grounded — supported, uncertainty explicit, no unsupported certainty?**
For: Eleven admitted excerpts, all official primary sources; the boundary panels explicitly refuse budget-availability, procurement, and vendor-intent claims; the procurement coverage gap is declared; the numeric tension ($20M vs $19.6M vs ~$18.33M) is now honestly annotated as unresolved rather than smoothed over.
Against: Three of eleven sources (Redtail ×2, UHAIV ×2 — four excerpts) carry "Not established" publication and currency dates yet feed thesis-level claims; the headerless Responsible AI table row is annotated but still means the single most load-bearing number has undetermined column semantics. Grounded, yes — but two of its anchors are weaker than the surface's polish suggests.

**3. Honest — facts, interpretations, questions, recommendations, omissions, contradictions represented truthfully?**
For: This is the proposal's strongest axis. The four-state grammar held under three adversarial reads; the known contradiction is displayed, not hidden; entity boundaries (U vs U Health vs USHE vs State) are kept clean, which pre-empts the classic account-research conflation failure.
Against: Nothing material. The stray trailing quote in the "refined the FTE plan." excerpt slightly dents the "exact wording" promise (note-and-carry, frozen artifact).

**4. Navigable — answer, exact support, evidence boundary findable without getting lost?**
For: Answers-first layout, per-statement evidence dialogs, relabeled "Related evidence context" where support is contextual, distinct accessible names, boundary panels on every dialog. The naive reader — zero Atliera context — called the account content fully self-explanatory.
Against: The coverage list, even collapsed, is ten rows for one gap; and the three funding streams require the reader to hold the reinvestment/Redtail/UHAIV distinction across sections.

**5. Worth continuing — is the next move safe and useful enough to Continue?**
For: The recommended move (discovery mapping owners, timelines, allowable uses, procurement path before positioning anything) is conservative, safe, and exactly what the procurement gap demands. Lineage is single-head (`2b8a296` for both execution and validation) — the clean C3 primary candidate by the retro's own recommendation.
Against: Only that the move is generic — any competent seller would do this. But at C2's maturity, safe-and-correct beats clever.

**Balance of evidence: Continue.** No reviewer in any pass found a defect in this proposal that survives the fix pass as a blocker. If you Continue with a note, the natural one is: elevate Redtail/UHAIV from "Still open" context toward the meaningful-change narrative in C3 prep, and treat the undated Redtail/UHAIV sources as recheck-first items.

## FedEx Corporation

**1. Useful — are the thesis and most meaningful change commercially useful?**
For: Network 2.0 with a hard public deadline (U.S. completion by end of calendar 2027) is a genuine, dated transformation surface; the digital-backbone/AI-orchestration language gives a legitimate hook for AI-workflow positioning; entity boundaries (parent vs Federal Express vs Freight spin-off vs Dataworks vs RouteSmart) are precise and commercially relevant.
Against: The thesis is close to a paraphrase of FedEx's own investor narrative — it tells a seller what FedEx *says about itself*, not anything the market doesn't already know. Both "Established" and "Meaningfully changed" are verbatim 10-K prose (now quoted and attributed, but still: the proposal's two headline facts are one document's self-description). Usefulness is real but thin relative to Utah.

**2. Grounded — supported, uncertainty explicit, no unsupported certainty?**
For: What it claims, it supports; the 10-K is the right primary source; the boundary panels refuse budget/procurement/intent claims; the "Still open" statement is admirably specific about what is unknown.
Against: Source concentration — the evidence base is dominated by a single filing plus governance charters, one of which (Cyber and Technology Oversight Committee Charter) carries "Not established" dates. Nine sources, but effectively one voice. Grounded within its base; the base itself is narrow.

**3. Honest — represented truthfully?**
For: The mixed-head limitation is now defined in plain language in the rubric and preserved rather than smoothed; first-person prose is attributed; no claim exceeds its excerpt.
Against: The `gaps_contradictions` taxonomy came back **empty** — a coverage gap, honestly declared, but it means the pipeline found no tension in an account where tension certainly exists (a company mid-restructuring with a spun-off segment and an unclosed Supply Chain sale has contradictions; the run just didn't surface them). Honest about its limits; the limit itself is a quality signal.

**4. Navigable — findable without getting lost?**
For: Same surface machinery as Utah; equally navigable.
Against: Nothing beyond the shared nits.

**5. Worth continuing — is the next move safe and useful enough to Continue?**
For: The move is bounded and safe; nothing in it commits, sends, or overclaims. The "Still open" questions are the right discovery agenda.
Against: The move's lead action — supplier-portal registration — is the weakest content in either proposal: the portal's own quoted caveat says contracts are multi-year and bids may be far off, so the recommendation leads with its lowest-yield, longest-horizon channel. The genuinely promising threads (Dataworks as the digital unit, the C&T Oversight Committee as the governance door, Network 2.0's 2027 deadline as the urgency frame) sit in the evidence but don't drive the recommendation. And the mixed-head limitation, while documented, is the reason the retro itself demoted FedEx to secondary C3 candidate.

**Balance of evidence: Revise before C3 — narrowly over Continue-with-limitation.** Nothing here is *wrong*; the question is whether C3 meeting prep built on this foundation would be load-bearing. The revision is small and specific: re-lead the next move with the Dataworks/orchestration and Network 2.0-deadline threads and demote portal registration to housekeeping; strengthen or explicitly re-scope the thesis beyond 10-K self-description; and (if the metered-budget proposal lands) let a fresh single-head execution both cure the mixed-head limitation and re-run gaps/contradictions. If you'd rather not spend a cycle, Continue-with-limitation is defensible — but then the C3 prep inherits a recommendation the evidence itself undercuts.
