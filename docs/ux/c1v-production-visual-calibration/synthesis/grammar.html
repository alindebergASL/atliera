The four-state grammar

  1. 01Established

    What is already held. Context that deserves attention, stated once and kept quiet.

  2. 02Meaningfully changed

    The one change that matters, bound to its exact support by an evidence stitch.

  3. 03Still open

    The uncertainty boundary. What the exact sources do not establish, held on the dark plane.

  4. 04Recommended next move

    One safe move offered as a hypothesis to review — never a conclusion or an action.

One numeral per state: the kicker badge is the only stage number. Reading guides and marginalia stay unnumbered so no stage is counted twice.

Composition rules

  1. Paper-bright canvas throughout; one dark plum decision plane holds Still open and Next together in the first desktop viewport.

  2. Mobile shows the account answer before any explanation of the system.

  3. Detailed evidence and lifecycle mechanics stay on demand behind the unchanged panel system.
