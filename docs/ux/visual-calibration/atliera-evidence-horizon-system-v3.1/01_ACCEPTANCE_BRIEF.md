# Acceptance brief

## Experience thesis

Atliera should feel like an exceptionally good account partner who has already done the work:

- It tells the user what matters now.
- It distinguishes what is established, what remains open, and what to do next.
- It makes exact support easy to inspect without forcing evidence mechanics into the default view.
- It uses AI to synthesize, compare, prioritize, and route exceptions—not to create a chatbot-first product or confidence theater.
- It asks for human judgment only when ambiguity, conflict, sensitivity, or external use makes judgment consequential.

The governing interaction rule remains:

> Answers first. Evidence on demand. Machinery by invitation.

## Expressive floor

Calm is not visual flattening. The four byte-preserved predecessor visual-generation v2 references remain binding for expressive ambition:

- Editorial Intelligence: an authored account narrative with proof connected to meaning and one composed next-move plane.
- Change Horizon: a recognizable relationship among established context, meaningful change or open state, and next action.
- Question Stage: natural-language information scent instead of internal ontology.
- Evidence Horizon: the evidence spine, question-led exploration, useful uncertainty, and persistent Account Workspace identity.

For C1, at least three qualities must be visually evident before deliberate exploration: an authored account story, evidence lineage attached to the consequential statement, and an `Established → Open → Next` decision orientation.

The v3 exact visual system still governs tokens, accessibility, responsiveness, evidence interaction, trust semantics, and milestone truth. The predecessor images do not authorize their generated shell controls, dynamic claims, or exact pixels.

## Account Workspace and Workshop continuity

The long-term account shell retains two deliberate modes:

- `Overview`: the effortless answer-first default.
- `Workshop`: the optional account-local place to turn understanding into work through question-led exploration, strategy, meeting preparation, evidence, and later approved briefings.

Workshop is not the M5b Package Inspector, the repository's current `/workshop` fake-runtime route, Signals/Maps/Plays Level 1 navigation, or a global object workspace. It must remain calm, task-specific, and progressively disclosed.

C1 does not implement or display Workshop. It must not ship a dead tab, placeholder, or route. It preserves the future extension seam while `Explore account` remains the functional read-only disclosure. The real Workshop appears only in a separately authorized slice with useful content and an applicable authorization boundary.

`visuals/04-prepare-composer-future-c3.png` is the binding calm function model for the later C3 Workshop: two initially visible inputs, context carried forward, optional depth behind `More options`, one predictable internal-draft result, and no implicit save, approval, share, or send.

## Inherit

- Immediate account identity and page purpose.
- One dominant account thesis or meaningful change.
- One bounded implication and one safe next move.
- Strong scan hierarchy, whitespace, readable line length, and editorial polish.
- A compact source/freshness/boundary cue close to consequential claims.
- An evidence spine that keeps the answer connected to its support.
- An Established → Open → Next horizon that organizes meaning without becoming a score.
- One primary action whose label predicts the result.
- Responsive recomposition that preserves the answer, boundary, and next action.
- AI presence through useful work already completed and clearly labeled draft synthesis.

## Avoid

- Generic CRM or BI dashboards with equal-weight cards, donut charts, and decorative AI sparkles.
- Salesforce-like navigation density, object trees, and all modules visible at once.
- Signals / Maps / Plays as default everyday information architecture.
- Repeated trust disclaimers, “pending” pills, and legalistic warning walls.
- Source registers, hashes, byte spans, package identities, effect counts, and audit language in Level 1.
- Per-item Accept / Reject controls for ordinary users.
- Chatbot-first interaction, model selectors, prompt boxes, and fake AI language.
- Turning a public amount into available spend, opportunity value, urgency, buying intent, or procurement.
- A giant metric hero that anchors the user on money while hiding funding stage and scope uncertainty.
- Calmness achieved by removing useful meaning or hiding every action.

## Level 1 content budget

The first view should normally contain:

- one thesis;
- one meaningful change;
- one implication;
- one proposed or safe next move;
- one compact trust/freshness line;
- one primary action;
- no more than three ranked secondary items behind deliberate exploration.

Absent or unsupported material is hidden rather than replaced with empty cards.

## Canonical University of Utah wording

Safe attributed statement:

> A University of Utah source says $4.94M is being redirected into artificial intelligence within a three-year reinvestment process.

Required nearby boundary:

> Remaining amount, detailed allocation, timing, procurement status, decision ownership, and vendor intent are not established by that source statement.

Safe possible implication:

> A decision window may be forming; execution status remains to be validated.

Safe next move:

> Verify the funding decision frame before solution positioning.

Prohibited upgrades include: “available budget,” “funding secured,” “approved spend,” “$4.94M opportunity,” “active procurement,” and “buying intent.”

## Milestone truth

- **C1:** deterministic local/test projection from already-admitted input. No monitoring, model work, persistence, new preparation, or approval.
- **C2:** governed model proposes a thesis, implication, and next move. Output is labeled, unreviewed, and non-durable.
- **C3:** user supplies exactly audience and intended outcome; Atliera creates a reviewable internal draft. Nothing is sent.
- **C4:** consequential exceptions can be reviewed and an approved internal snapshot can be frozen. Audience approval and delivery remain separate.
- **C7:** authorized researcher detail may expose custody and lineage behind deliberate disclosure.
- **C9:** recurring checks, meaningful-change detection, and Today prioritization. No C1 screen may imply this capability.

## Trust semantics

Never collapse these dimensions into one badge:

1. Origin and support: unknown, attributed only, or admitted exact support.
2. Content kind: source fact, interpretation, question, or recommendation.
3. Review: draft, needs review, or reviewed.
4. Freshness: source date, independently verified current-through date, stale, or superseded.
5. Durability and snapshot: ephemeral, not yet frozen, or reviewed snapshot.
6. Audience approval: not approved or approved for a named audience and use.
7. Delivery: not shared, shared, or published.
8. Attention: ordinary or conflict requiring judgment.

A source date is not automatically an “evidence current through” date. Source support does not imply human review. AI generation does not imply verification.

## Meeting preparation

The ordinary future Prepare journey asks only:

1. Who is this for?
2. What outcome do you want?

Atliera carries the current account boundaries into the draft and turns unknowns into neutral questions. Strategy is optional and may add value, but it must not become a mandatory tollgate before preparation.

For the Utah scenario, good questions test funding stage, scope, owner, constraints, timing, process, and success measures. They do not ask, “How will you spend the $4.94M?”

## Critical acceptance gates

Any failure is a HOLD:

- A first-time user can explain account, change, implication, unknowns, and next move within 60 seconds.
- The truthful primary action is discoverable within 15 seconds.
- Exact support is reachable from a consequential claim in no more than two deliberate interactions.
- No Level 1 screen exposes Package Inspector machinery or researcher identifiers.
- No trust label implies support, review, freshness, AI work, approval, or delivery without that exact state.
- No source statement is converted into opportunity value or buying intent.
- No horizontal overflow, clipping, priority inversion, or loss of the trust boundary on mobile.
- The implementation meets WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard, focus-return, reduced-motion, and 200–400% reflow requirements.
- Sparse, stale, conflict, and safe-failure states remain useful and honest.

## Originality gate

With the Atliera name hidden, reviewers should still identify an evidence-aware account-intelligence and preparation workspace, not a generic CRM. At least two product-specific behaviors should be obvious: for example, meaningful-change prioritization, evidence lineage, exception-only review, or an audience-ready next move.
