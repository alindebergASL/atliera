## User-visible correction

A saved brief can retain valid historical custody without being the exact initial record for the currently configured replay pair. Such a brief must explain that no recorded revision is available, keep revision controls disabled through interaction, and remain readable and annotatable. The service refuses both new staging and restored pending generation for an unmatched pair before provider work or generation-operation state changes. Exact replay and independently configured live generation retain their existing boundaries.

After explicit Apply, the client adopts the server's revised availability and explanation in place. The old fixed-instruction selector becomes disabled rather than offering another nonexistent response. Handler-bound controls, notes, selected section and local drafts are preserved. Applying, noting, saving and reopening do not silently change provenance or repair a model response.
