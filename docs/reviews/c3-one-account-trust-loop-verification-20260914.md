## Bounded behavior covered

The [existing trust-loop runbook](../runbooks/c3-account-information-validation.md) describes the combined candidate's contracts and limits:

- **Exact conflict judgments:** resolve selected existing contradictions against the exact current proposition, entity and time, with an attributed reason and specific factual basis from attached evidence or an honest firsthand basis. Restoration names one exact currently effective contradiction/resolution pair and its reason; other and later conflicts remain separate. Neither action grants validation, corroboration or acceptance of a refused generation. Original evidence, wording, scope and judgments remain in immutable history.
- **Attention across edits:** changed wording starts a new statement revision, invalidates active support/validation/time assessments and expires prior resolutions. Historical conflicts retain their original scope and require review for the edited proposition; they are not proof against unrelated wording. Expired judgments cannot be restored as active. Reopen changes attention without reversing or reapplying a resolution. No-change preserves the version and schema.
- **As-saved evidence:** legacy schema 1/2 snapshots retain their original revision-local labels, wording and hashes. Current Account review is shown separately, including when its interpretation differs at the same stored version. Publishing a review, choosing **Refresh working snapshot**, and explicitly **Save** produces an updated saved version; neither refresh nor adding information automatically saves the brief. Historical prose, records and snapshots are not silently rewritten.
- **Useful navigation:** account-local literal search filters rendered information and evidence, preserves typed reasoning and announces match counts. **Return to brief and Save** resumes the existing account-qualified session draft after an addition.
- **Constrained trial labeling:** the optional private `serve-information` preview metadata requires a 40-character lowercase hexadecimal configured build SHA and the fixed mode `Isolated operator preview · retained evidence · generation and acquisition off`. The operator must independently verify that SHA. Header/health display is configuration, not a runtime measurement, signature, authentication, factual confirmation or new permission. Historical replay remains distinct from fresh verification.
- **Prospective restoration capacity:** every changed publication preserves one future restore entry per active resolved target, including judgments covering several targets. History plus remaining reservations must fit the 100-entry limit; a shared-root 4,090-entry publication budget preserves reservations across accounts/operators; the 2,000,000-byte envelope reserves space for every remaining restore with maximum legal reason length, JSON escaping, attribution and timestamp. Unrelated writes cannot spend reserved capacity. Exact restoration consumes an entry and releases its reservation; edits expire judgments and release reservations; reopen does not. Unsafe writes refuse before publication and retain typed reasoning. These checks preserve logical capacity for admitted writes without quota expansion, migration or deletion; they do not repair previously stranded diagnostic records or guarantee writes through physical storage failures.
