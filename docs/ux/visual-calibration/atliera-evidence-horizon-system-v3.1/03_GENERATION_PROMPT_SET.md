# Generation prompt set

Mode: built-in image generation and image editing, with supplied Atliera references used for visual continuity.

These are the final prompt intents and non-negotiable constraints. Exact visual copy is governed by the PNGs and `01_ACCEPTANCE_BRIEF.md`.

## Shared visual language

Generate production-grade Atliera product concepts using warm Mineral Paper, Evergreen Black ink, restrained Cobalt action, Eucalyptus evidence, Apricot open questions, and Oxide conflict. Combine refined editorial serif headlines with a clean humanist sans UI. Use purposeful whitespace, minimal elevation, thin evidence lineage, and an Established → Open → Next horizon. Avoid generic CRM grids, decorative AI, gradients, glassmorphism, dense sidebars, tiny legal text, and audit machinery in everyday views.

Always label current versus future milestone behavior. Preserve the attributed $4.94M University of Utah statement and the Jun 19, 2026 source date. Never imply remaining cash, available seller budget, procurement, opportunity value, buying intent, or vendor intent.

## Preserved expressive lineage

Future Atliera concepts must retain the strongest qualities of the four predecessor visual-generation v2 references without copying their pixels or unsupported claims:

- Editorial Intelligence: premium asymmetric account storytelling, proof connected to the narrative, and a composed next-move plane.
- Change Horizon: an ownable Established → Open → Next relationship; reserve detected-change and “since last view” semantics for C9.
- Question Stage: natural-language account exploration and useful unresolved questions.
- Evidence Horizon: evidence spine, question-led exploration, change orientation, and a persistent-workspace feeling.

Do not flatten Atliera into a generic white account page or conventional card dashboard. Calmness must preserve scale, pacing, depth, material contrast, and recognizable evidence-aware product behavior.

Retain `Workshop` as a future account-local task mode, but never render a dead tab or unauthorized function. `visuals/04-prepare-composer-future-c3.png` is the model for later calm Workshop functions; none of its C3 controls exist in C1.
