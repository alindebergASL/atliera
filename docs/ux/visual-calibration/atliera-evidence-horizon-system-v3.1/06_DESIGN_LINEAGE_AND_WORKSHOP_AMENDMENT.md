# Design lineage and Workshop contract

This amendment prevents the calm-product pivot from flattening Atliera into a merely clean or minimal account dashboard.

## The governing synthesis

Atliera inherits two complementary layers:

1. **Expressive identity from the four v2 concepts** — premium composition, visual tension, editorial confidence, a memorable change horizon, question-led exploration, and evidence visibly connected to meaning.
2. **Product discipline from Evidence Horizon System v3** — zero-training hierarchy, honest lifecycle semantics, responsive behavior, complete states, accessibility, evidence on demand, and exact implementation boundaries.

Neither layer replaces the other.

> v2 supplies the emotional and distinctive product language. v3 supplies the behavioral, trust, and production system.

Calm means compressed decision value. It does not mean removing the visual moments that make Atliera feel intelligent, differentiated, or alive.

## Binding expressive references

The exact four preserved files under `lineage/v2-owner-references/` are owner-approved expressive calibration. In this packet, “predecessor visual-generation v2” refers only to the earlier visual-exploration packet; it never means Atliera milestone C2 Background Intelligence.

### 1. Editorial Intelligence

Inherit:

- the premium editorial first impression;
- asymmetric composition instead of an equal-weight card grid;
- a thesis that feels connected to change, implication, proof, and next action;
- sentence-level evidence markers stitched into the story;
- one decisive next-move surface.

Do not inherit unproven reviewed-context language, unnecessary navigation density, or any implication that the page is a one-time briefing rather than a persistent account workspace.

### 2. Change Horizon

Inherit:

- the recognizable relationship among established context, meaningful change or open state, and the next safe action;
- a visual sense of continuity and movement;
- meaningful change as a first-class Atliera product moment;
- background work communicated through the usefulness of the result rather than exposed machinery.

The horizon must encode real, available state. C1 uses the truthful `Established → Open → Next` orientation. Recurring `Known → Changed → Next`, “since your last view,” completed background checks, detected-change counts, and other delta semantics require C9 to have actually run.

### 3. Question Stage

Inherit:

- natural-language exploration: what should I know, what changed, why it matters, what remains open, and what should I do next;
- an answer stage with strong focus and calm depth;
- unresolved questions presented as useful account intelligence rather than a warning wall;
- an intuitive bridge from understanding into account work.

Do not turn the questions into a chatbot, prompt interface, mandatory wizard, or one-answer-at-a-time tunnel that hides the overall account story.

### 4. Evidence Horizon — recommended synthesis

Inherit:

- the evidence spine connecting thesis, change, implication, and next move;
- the compact change horizon;
- question-led exploration;
- the balance of premium editorial space and persistent-workspace utility;
- a visible exception only when something consequential needs attention.

This remains the strongest compositional reference. Evidence Horizon System v3 refines its trust copy, states, accessibility, responsiveness, and milestone truth; it does not reject its visual direction.

If “calm” and “wow” appear to conflict, preserve the information hierarchy and reduce decoration, controls, and repetition before removing the distinctive evidence, change, or question-led product grammar.

## Explicitly nonbinding predecessor elements

Do not inherit exact pixels, generated icons, decorative waves or gradients, the full-height purple answer stage, or the predecessor global navigation. Do not inherit claims such as `Background intelligence complete`, `since your last view`, reviewed context, updated-item counts, monitoring, AI activity, current-through state, or durable account state unless the exact milestone and proof exist. Do not show `View meeting plan` without an already-admitted plan.

The predecessor visuals do not authorize search, notifications, account switchers, profile controls, source registers, extra status counts, or secondary modules that lack real local/test behavior.

## Account Workspace information architecture

Atliera retains **Workshop** as a first-class, account-local mode.

The durable product model is:

### Overview

The effortless default for an ordinary user:

- account thesis;
- meaningful change;
- implication;
- unknown or exception when material;
- one safe next move;
- evidence on demand.

### Workshop

The deliberate place to turn account intelligence into useful work:

- explore the account by familiar questions;
- inspect strategy and decision landscape when needed;
- view or create audience-specific preparation when the relevant milestone exists;
- work with meeting plans and later approved briefing snapshots;
- reach evidence and researcher depth through progressive disclosure and authorization.

Workshop is not a return to Signals / Maps / Plays as Level 1 navigation. It is not a Salesforce-style object workspace, a page full of modules, or an always-visible research console.

## Tab and control rule

The intended account shell has two clear modes: `Overview` and `Workshop`.

- `Overview` is the default.
- `Workshop` is deliberate and task-oriented.
- A live tab or control must always lead to real, permitted content; never ship a dead or misleading tab.
- The current C1 slice does not implement Workshop. Do not render a `Workshop` tab, disabled placeholder, `Coming soon` item, or dead link in C1.
- C1 retains its functional `Explore account` disclosure for no more than three admitted secondary items and must preserve a clean future extension seam for an account-local Workshop.
- Do not point a future Workshop affordance at the existing `/workshop` fake-runtime route or the M5b Package Inspector.
- Surface Workshop only in the first separately authorized slice that supplies a real destination, useful content, responsive behavior, and an applicable authorization boundary.
- The real `Prepare for…` composer belongs to C3.

## Prepare composer inheritance

`visuals/04-prepare-composer-future-c3.png` is the owner-approved model for how a Workshop function should feel:

- a focused task rather than a dense configuration page;
- exactly two initially visible decisions: audience and intended outcome;
- relevant account context and uncertainty carried in automatically;
- optional depth behind `More options`;
- one clear internal-draft action;
- explicit no-save/no-send boundary until the appropriate durable and delivery milestones exist;
- no model selector, prompt engineering, evidence register, or approval machinery in the ordinary flow.

Workshop functions should reuse this pattern: **context already assembled, minimal human input, one predictable result, evidence and machinery available only when invited.**

## Implementation quality gate

A C1 implementation fails even if it is clean and usable when it:

- looks like a generic account-summary dashboard;
- loses the evidence spine, meaningful-change grammar, question-led information scent, or a distinctive next-move moment;
- treats v2 as obsolete mood-board work;
- treats Workshop as removed from the product;
- copies v2 capability claims that C1 cannot prove;
- introduces a nonfunctional Workshop or Prepare control;
- uses calmness to justify an empty first viewport.

The masked-brand originality test must identify Atliera as an evidence-aware account-intelligence and preparation workspace, with at least two of these recognizable behaviors: connected evidence, meaningful-change horizon, question-led exploration, exception-only review, and an audience-ready next move.
